Meet Chucky

Chucky

The founder. The fryer. The legend.

Chucky never set out to build a restaurant. He just loved fried chicken, and he loved watching people’s faces light up when they tried it.

He believed great chicken was too good to keep to himself. Friends, neighbours and anyone walking by got a plate, and every bite came with a story and a grin.

Today, Chucky’s Chikn is that passion with a door and a menu. Every batch is fried to share Chucky’s love of chicken with the world, starting right here in Montréal.

“Love makes the heart want chicken.”
